# ENTITY Data Economic Sovereignty Doctrine

Status: ENTITY v3.1.0 RELEASED / NORMATIVE DOCTRINE

## Doctrine

Data may represent economically valuable productive capital.

ENTITY SHALL permit data, information, observations, datasets, corpora, derived information and other informational assets to be represented as Digital Commodity Objects (DCOs).

Economic value SHALL NOT require artificial scarcity of information itself. The information may remain non-rival and technically copyable while economically scarce interests are expressed separately.

Scarcity MAY arise through explicitly defined, machine-verifiable rights, entitlements, capacities, durations, jurisdictions, usage quantities, derivation rights, participation rights, transferability limits and other bounded economic interests.

Such interests MAY be issued, reserved, licensed, transferred, traded, cleared, settled, exercised, revoked, superseded or extinguished according to their governing terms.

The originator MAY retain continuing economic participation in primary issuance, secondary exchange, derivative creation or subsequent economic use where those rights were validly established.

ENTITY SHALL preserve provenance between the underlying information, the rights governing it, the uses made of it, derived outputs and resulting economic consequences.

The protocol itself SHALL NOT determine legal ownership, legal title, objective external payment truth, accounting fair value, market fair value or regulatory classification. Those remain dependent upon applicable law, authoritative evidence, external systems and jurisdiction.

## Economic object boundary

The underlying bytes are not the market instrument merely because they are registered as a DCO. A tradable EEP instrument MUST identify the bounded RIGHT or entitlement being issued and MUST NOT silently transfer ownership of underlying information.

An economic interest MUST have machine-readable bounds sufficient to distinguish it from unrestricted possession. Bounds may include action, quantity, duration, territory, derivation scope, transferability, capacity or participation terms.

Originator participation MUST be explicit, disclosed, effective-dated and attributable to valid policy/contract semantics. ENTITY SHALL NOT infer a royalty, reserve or participation interest merely from creation of a DCO.

## ENTITY integration

The doctrine is implemented through existing ENTITY layers rather than a separate token system:

- Universal Transaction Fabric: DCO identity, authority, RIGHT, EVENT, VALUE and provenance.
- EEP: instruments, disclosures, listings, orders, execution, clearing, settlement, entitlements and usage.
- EOPP: issuer-defined reserve, primary, secondary, derivative and service participation without protocol tax or mandatory token.
- Attribution methodology: evidence-backed contribution methodologies without pretending percentages are universal truth.
- Market recovery: provider-independent survival of balances, trades, entitlements, settlement evidence and economic bindings.
- Global infrastructure profiles: jurisdiction, ontology, privacy, governance, topology and cryptographic migration semantics.

## Strategic description

ENTITY is not merely a stock market for data. It is market infrastructure through which data can become measurable economic capital while remaining governed by explicit rights rather than artificial byte scarcity.

A concise description is:

> ENTITY transforms data from an opaque digital possession into a provenance-backed economic asset whose rights can be defined, issued, exchanged, exercised and economically accounted for.

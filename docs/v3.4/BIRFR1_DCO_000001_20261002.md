# DCO-000001 — Blackmore Industrial Robotics Failure & Recovery Dataset (BIRFR-1)

Date: 2026-10-02

Asset version: **0.1.0**

## Why this is DCO-000001

BIRFR-1 is the first numbered Blackmore DCO built around a real underlying BTG-originated asset rather than economic structure alone.

Its purpose is to represent the sequence:

`NORMAL EXECUTION → FAILURE → RECOVERY`

as a first-class robotics training and evaluation unit.

The dataset is aimed at failure detection, failure classification, recovery selection, recovery-policy learning, anomaly detection, industrial safety evaluation and cross-embodiment research.

## Qualified underlying asset

BIRFR-1 v0.1.0 contains **100,000 deterministic synthetic episodes**:

- 30,000 success-only;
- 30,000 failure-only;
- 30,000 successful recovery;
- 10,000 failed/alternative recovery.

It covers **20 failure families**, **25 industrial task families**, and **5 embodiment classes**.

The payload is stored in **100 multimodal shards** plus a SQLite catalog.

## Modalities

The qualified schema includes RGB, depth, wrist RGB, joint positions, joint velocities, joint torques, end-effector pose, gripper state, force/torque, robot actions, natural-language task, task phase, failure class/timestamp, root cause, recovery strategy/result and safety/near-miss flags.

## Cross-embodiment design

Task intent, failure state, corrective intent and recovery result are represented separately from embodiment-specific joint/state fields.

The benchmark includes a dedicated cross-embodiment holdout and a dedicated unknown-failure holdout.

## Blackmore Recovery Benchmark

The current benchmark defines:

- TRAIN — 70,000 episodes
- VAL — 10,000
- TEST_KNOWN — 10,000
- TEST_UNKNOWN_FAILURE — 5,000
- TEST_CROSS_EMBODIMENT — 5,000

The scorer covers failure detection/classification, time-to-detection, recovery selection, task completion after recovery, unsafe-action rate, false-recovery rate, unknown failure/object/environment generalization and cross-robot transfer.

A 20,000-test-episode ground-truth self-test validated the scorer implementation. That self-test validates scoring logic; it is not a trained-model performance result.

## Important visual-data boundary

The v0.1.0 RGB/depth/wrist snapshots are compact **16×16 procedural synthetic qualification imagery**.

They are real BTG-generated asset bytes and qualify the multimodal data path, but this release does **not** claim photorealistic, high-resolution or real-factory camera fidelity.

High-fidelity simulation and real-hardware episodes belong in later versioned BIRFR releases rather than being silently mixed into v0.1.0.

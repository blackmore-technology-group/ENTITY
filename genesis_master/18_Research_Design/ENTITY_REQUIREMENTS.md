# ENTITY Research / Design Requirements
Source: SERS-ENTITY-003 Complete Master Engineering Design v2.2
Status: Authoritative repository requirement mapping

## Research Scope
- Research artifacts SHALL distinguish proposed design, experiment, validated evidence and production-qualified capability.
- Standards research SHOULD prioritize interoperability before proprietary reinvention.
- Prior-art and patent research SHALL remain separate from assertions of ownership or validity.

## Design Principles
- Research SHALL preserve the invariant that cryptographic evidence is not automatically legal ownership, factual truth or market value.
- New valuation models SHALL distinguish potential, offer, contracted, accrued, settled and realized value.
- New identity mechanisms SHALL preserve stable sovereign identity across key/algorithm changes.
- Privacy research SHOULD support selective disclosure, pairwise identifiers and privacy-preserving proofs/matching.
- Data-space research SHOULD cover controlled access, clean rooms, computation-to-data and auditable usage.

## Promotion to Production
- Experimental mechanisms SHALL require explicit qualification before becoming authoritative runtime behavior.
- Unknown or unresolved technical/legal assumptions SHALL be documented rather than fabricated as established facts.

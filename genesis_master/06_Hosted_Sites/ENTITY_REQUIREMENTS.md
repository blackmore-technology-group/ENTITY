# ENTITY Hosted Sites Requirements
Source: SERS-ENTITY-003 Complete Master Engineering Design v2.2
Status: Authoritative repository requirement mapping

## Scope
- Hosted sites SHALL remain governed Entity assets with provenance, rights, policy and export state.
- Private and published sites SHALL be treated as different disclosure states.

## Requirements
- Site content and static assets SHALL receive Asset/AssetVersion identities where governed.
- Publication SHALL create explicit export/publication evidence without rewriting source ownership or provenance.
- Site templates and generated derivatives SHOULD preserve parent/ingredient lineage.
- Public publication SHALL respect permissions, prohibitions, consent, platform terms and approval thresholds.
- Sensitive content and private paths SHALL not be exposed merely because the hosting subsystem can access them.
- Where third-party code, media or SDKs are included, applicable rights/licence obligations SHALL be represented.
- Site data retention and deletion SHALL follow policy-specific lifecycle rules.

## User Protection
- Broad, perpetual, exclusive, AI-training or sublicensing effects SHALL be surfaced before publication when applicable.
- Unknown rights or terms SHALL be marked unresolved/review-required rather than assumed safe.

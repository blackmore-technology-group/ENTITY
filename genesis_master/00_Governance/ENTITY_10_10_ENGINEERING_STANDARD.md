# ENTITY 10/10 Engineering Standard
Status: Authoritative engineering target
Date adopted: 2026-09-15

ENTITY earns a 10/10 engineering claim only through reproducible evidence. Architecture, documentation, code presence or unit tests alone are insufficient.

## Mandatory Gates
1. **Architecture** — every canonical authority has an explicit owner, ABI, invariants, failure behavior and versioned schema.
2. **Sovereignty** — identity, recovery, export and historical evidence survive loss of optional BTG/cloud/AI/payment infrastructure.
3. **Privacy** — default-deny source enrollment, minimum-necessary AI projection, pairwise identity and metadata minimization are adversarially qualified.
4. **Security** — root takeover, signature bypass, rights mutation, vault disclosure, agent escalation, connector escalation, replay and prompt injection are attacked and blocked.
5. **AI Governance** — NIKI reasoning is non-authoritative; ADAM execution requires bounded capability and required approvals.
6. **Rights / Provenance** — claims, evidence origins, provenance, rights verification and disputes remain distinct and independently verifiable.
7. **Economics** — potential, offered, contracted, accrued, settled and realized value remain distinct; accounting balances and external payment evidence are verified.
8. **Interoperability** — supported standards are tested by export, independent validation, import and semantic equivalence.
9. **Resilience** — backup restore, offline operation, dependency failure, reconciliation and destructive recovery are demonstrated.
10. **Qualification** — fresh end-to-end scenarios pass without manual database edits and produce sealed machine-readable evidence.

## Claim Rule
The release gate SHALL fail if any mandatory gate lacks current evidence or any critical defect remains unresolved.
Subsystem TRLs and scores SHALL be reported independently when maturity differs.

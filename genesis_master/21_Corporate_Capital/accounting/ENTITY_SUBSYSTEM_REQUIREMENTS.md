# ENTITY Capital Accounting Reconciliation Requirements
Source: SERS-ENTITY-003 Section 168

- External capital-accounting journals SHALL require verified external accounting authority evidence.
- Journal snapshots SHALL balance debits and credits independently by currency before adoption.
- Accounting entries SHALL remain semantically distinct from legal share ownership and shareholder-register state.
- Accounting entries SHALL NOT prove external cash movement.
- When external cash consideration is required, reconciliation SHALL require a final provider-confirmed external settlement and verify amount, currency and issuer settlement role.
- Replay of an adopted external accounting batch SHALL be rejected.

# ENTITY Instrument Classification Requirements
Source: SERS-ENTITY-003 Sections 163–164

- Jurisdiction and instrument-action policy SHALL be versioned and effective-dated.
- Unknown or unconfigured jurisdiction/action combinations SHALL fail closed to authorized review rather than automatic execution.
- Engineering classifications SHALL NOT be represented as legal determinations.
- Historical evidence SHALL retain the policy version and authority reference applicable at the event time.
- A configured DENY SHALL prevent adoption of the action into authoritative capital evidence.
- A configured ALLOW_EVIDENCE_ONLY SHALL permit evidence recording/reconciliation only and SHALL NOT enable broker, exchange, custody or transfer-agent execution.

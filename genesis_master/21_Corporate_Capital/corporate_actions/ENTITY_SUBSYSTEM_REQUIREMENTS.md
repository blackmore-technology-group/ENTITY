# ENTITY Corporate Action Reconciliation Requirements
Source: SERS-ENTITY-003 Sections 159, 163, 167–170

- Corporate actions SHALL require explicit corporate authority plus permitted jurisdiction/instrument policy.
- External corporate-action evidence SHALL be signature-verified and bound to the exact issuer, share class, action type and resulting capitalization snapshot.
- A valid external signature for one action SHALL NOT be replayed to justify another action or capitalization result.
- ENTITY MAY adopt an externally authoritative post-action capitalization snapshot but SHALL NOT claim that core ENTITY executed the legally effective action.
- Adopted capitalization SHALL reconcile authorized, issued, outstanding and shareholder-position quantities.
- Corporate action records SHALL be replay-resistant and independently retrievable for qualification.

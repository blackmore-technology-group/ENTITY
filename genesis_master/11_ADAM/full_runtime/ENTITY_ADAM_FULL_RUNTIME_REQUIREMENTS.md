# ENTITY / Full ADAM v1.0 Integration Requirements

Status: ENTITY 2.0 development integration requirement.
Protocol 1.0 remains frozen and is not silently reinterpreted by this work.

## Authority separation

- ENTITY remains authoritative for identity, delegation, permissions, rights, policy, consent, contracts, usage and sovereign portability.
- ADAM owns deterministic atomic information representation, exact evidence, reaction-governed state transition, history, reconstruction, aligned semantic claims, custody/reference authority mechanisms and ADAM runtime evidence.
- NIKI reasons over bounded projections and proposes actions; NIKI does not acquire ENTITY or ADAM authority.
- BSIE remains authoritative for spatial/world representation within its governed scope.

## Full ADAM integration

- The canonical integration SHALL pin the verified inner signed ADAM v1.0 RC2 release by SHA-256.
- A modified outer wrapper SHALL NOT become the trust root when the signed inner release remains independently hash-verifiable.
- ENTITY-authorized transitions SHALL be mirrored into ADAM exact evidence and reaction history.
- ADAM SHALL reject authoritative commits when current ENTITY authorization cannot be verified.
- Exact evidence SHALL be reconstructable byte-for-byte.
- Semantic transition claims SHALL remain linked to exact evidence through ADAM evidence-alignment proofs.
- NIKI projections SHALL be metadata-minimized and SHALL NOT transfer source authority.
- Historical ADAM evidence SHALL not be rewritten during correction; superseding transitions SHALL be used.

## Qualification boundary

The integrated software may qualify the bounded software bridge. It SHALL NOT claim that ADAM external certification gates (HSM/KMS, physical multi-host, certified devices, licensed real-world training, thirty wall-clock days, independent audit) have passed unless the corresponding independent evidence exists.
